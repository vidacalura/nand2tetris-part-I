# Nand2Tetris - Part I

[Nand2Tetris](https://www.nand2tetris.org/) is a free website consisting of the content from the book The Elements of Computing Systems, By Noam Nisan and Shimon Schocken, which has as its goal 
to teach the fundamentals of computer architecture by building a computer from the ground up. The idea is to make a fully functioning primitive computer through a series of exercises, starting 
from the construction of a primitive Nand gate.

This repository contains my solutions to said projects. They are not official solutions, and by no means the "best" solutions. Still, feel free to check them if you so desire.
